The Ultimate Cheat Sheet

Everything you need for the AP Physics 1 Exam (Units 1-8)

UNIT 1 10-15%

Kinematics

Motion in 1D and 2D. Sketch axes → List knowns → Pick equation.

v = v₀ + at
Δx = v₀t + ½at²
v² = v₀² + 2aΔx
TRAP: "Slowing down" ≠ negative acceleration. Check signs relative to velocity!
UNIT 2 18-23%

Dynamics

Newton's Laws and Forces. Always draw FBDs first.

ΣF = ma
F_fric ≤ μF_n
F_g = mg
TRAP: Normal Force (N) isn't always mg. On a ramp, N = mg cosθ.
UNIT 3 18-23%

Work & Energy

Conservation of Energy and Power. Path irrelevant for gravity.

W = Fd cosθ
K = ½mv²
ΔE = W = ΔK + ΔU
TRAP: If velocity doubles, KE quadruples (KE ∝ v²).
UNIT 4 10-15%

Linear Momentum

Impulse and Collisions. Momentum is a VECTOR.

p = mv
J = FΔt = Δp
m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = m₁v₁' + m₂v₂'
TRAP: Opposite directions must have opposite signs (+ vs -).
UNIT 5 10-15%

Torque & Rotation

Rotational kinematics and inertia. CCW is positive.

τ = rF sinθ
L = Iω
Στ = Iα
TRAP: Skater pulls arms in → I decreases → ω increases (L is conserved).
UNIT 6 5-8%

Oscillations

Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM). Energy sloshing back/forth.

T_s = 2π√(m/k)
T_p = 2π√(L/g)
TRAP: Period (T) is independent of amplitude. Mass doesn't affect pendulums!
UNIT 7 7-12%

Gravitation

Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation and Orbits.

F_g = G(m₁m₂/r²)
g = G(M/r²)
TRAP: Distance (r) is from center-to-center, not surface-to-surface.
UNIT 8 10-15%

Fluids

Density, Pressure, Buoyancy, and Flow.

P = P₀ + ρgh
F_b = ρVg
A₁v₁ = A₂v₂
TRAP: Faster flow = Lower pressure (Bernoulli's Principle).
