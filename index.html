The Elastic Collision

A sphere of mass 2M moves with speed V toward a stationary sphere of mass M. After a perfectly elastic collision, the first sphere moves with speed V/3.

Task: Derive the final speed of the second sphere and justify why Kinetic Energy is conserved.

Scoring Rubric (4 Points Total)

  • 1pt: Statement of Conservation of Momentum (Σp_i = Σp_f).
  • 1pt: Correct algebraic setup: (2M)(V) + 0 = (2M)(V/3) + (M)(v_f2).
  • 1pt: Solving for v_f2 = 4/3 V.
  • 1pt: Explaining that elastic collisions, by definition, conserve total system Kinetic Energy.
