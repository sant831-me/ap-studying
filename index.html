UNIT 110-15%

Kinematics

v = v₀ + at
Δx = v₀t + ½at²
v² = v₀² + 2aΔx

Core Concepts

  • Projectiles: x-motion is constant velocity; y-motion is free fall.
  • Graphs: x-t slope = v; v-t slope = a; v-t area = Δx.
TRAP: At peak height, vertical velocity is 0, but acceleration is STILL -9.8 m/s².
UNIT 218-23%

Dynamics

ΣF = ma
F_fric ≤ μF_n
N_ramp = mg cosθ

FBD & Forces

  • Newton's 3rd: Action/Reaction pairs never act on the same object.
  • Inclines: Parallel force is mg sinθ; Perpendicular is mg cosθ.
TRAP: Tension in a moving Atwood machine is NOT equal to the weight of the masses.
UNIT 318-23%

Work & Energy

W = Fd cosθ
K = ½mv²
U_s = ½kx²

Conservation

  • Work-Energy: Net work equals change in Kinetic Energy.
  • Power: P = ΔE/Δt or P = Fv.
TRAP: Force perpendicular to motion (like Centripetal Force) does ZERO work.
UNIT 410-15%

Momentum

p = mv
J = FΔt = Δp
Σp_i = Σp_f

Impulse & Collisions

  • Elastic: Both p and KE are conserved.
  • Inelastic: Objects stick or deform; KE is lost to heat.
TRAP: Momentum is a vector! A bounce from -v to +v is a change of 2mv.
UNIT 510-15%

Rotation

τ = rF sinθ
L = Iω
Στ = Iα

Torque & Inertia

  • Inertia (I): Depends on mass distribution. Further mass = higher I.
  • Rolling: Total KE = Translation KE + Rotation KE.
TRAP: Angular momentum is conserved unless an external Torque acts.
UNIT 65-8%

Oscillations

T_s = 2π√(m/k)
T_p = 2π√(L/g)

Simple Harmonic Motion

  • Amplitude: Does NOT affect the period of a spring or pendulum.
  • Energy: Swaps between max PE at edges and max KE at center.
TRAP: Pendulum period is independent of mass.
UNIT 77-12%

Gravitation

F_g = G(m₁m₂/r²)
g = G(M/r²)

Orbits & Fields

  • Inverse Square: Double the distance, Force becomes 1/4th.
  • Weightlessness: Occurs when in free fall, not because g is zero.
TRAP: 'r' is distance from centers, not the surface of the planet.
UNIT 810-15%

Fluids

P = P₀ + ρgh
F_b = ρVg
A₁v₁ = A₂v₂

Fluid Mechanics

  • Buoyancy: Force equals the weight of the displaced fluid.
  • Bernoulli: High speed fluid = Low pressure.
TRAP: Floating objects displace their own Weight; Sunk objects displace their Volume.
